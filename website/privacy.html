Privacy policy

Last updated 18 November 2026

FlixView is built to keep what you watch to yourself. There is no account, no tracking and no advertising. This policy explains what the FlixView desktop app and this website do with information, and what other services they connect to.

The short version

  • FlixView does not ask for your name, email address or any account details.
  • Your watchlist, favourites and watch history are stored on your own computer. We never receive them.
  • The app has no analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • To show titles and play them, the app connects to third-party services, which receive the technical details that any internet request carries, such as your IP address.

Who we are

FlixView is a free, open-source desktop app for Windows, macOS and Linux, published by the FlixView project ("FlixView", "we" or "us"). This policy covers the FlixView app and this website.

You can contact us through GitHub Issues on the FlixView GitHub page.

What the app keeps on your computer

The app saves the following in its data folder on your computer, so it can remember your place and your lists:

  • your watchlist and favourites;
  • your watch progress and history, such as the episode you reached and how far into it you were;
  • app preferences, such as window size and position and whether the sidebar is collapsed.

This information never leaves your computer unless you choose to export it. In Settings you can open the data folder, export a backup, import one, and clear your watchlist, favourites or history, or everything at once.

Exported backups are ordinary files. Keep them somewhere safe, because anyone with the file can see your lists and history.

What we do not collect

  • We do not create accounts or collect names, email addresses or passwords.
  • The app contains no analytics, telemetry or tracking, and no advertising identifiers.
  • We do not sell, rent or share personal information, because we do not hold any.

Services the app connects to

FlixView works by connecting to other services. When it does, those services receive the technical information that comes with any internet request, such as your IP address and basic details about your device and software. Each service handles that information under its own privacy policy.

  • TMDB (The Movie Database) supplies film and series details and artwork.
  • AniList supplies anime details and artwork.
  • The video player provider (currently VidRift) supplies the player and the streams. The player runs inside the app and may use its own storage. FlixView clears the player's stored data before each title.
  • GitHub hosts the app's downloads and updates, which the app checks for when it starts and every 30 minutes while it is open, and the filter lists used by the built-in ad blocker.

The built-in blocker stops known advertising and tracking requests from the player and other pages. It works from public filter lists, so it cannot promise to catch everything.

This website

This website is hosted by Cloudflare. Like any host, Cloudflare processes technical information about requests, such as IP addresses, to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.

While you browse, your browser fetches the latest release details from GitHub and film and series artwork from TMDB. To avoid asking again on every visit, it keeps a copy in your browser's local storage for up to six hours. This stays on your device and is not a cookie.

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising.

Donations

If you choose to support the project, your donation is handled by PayPal or Buy Me a Coffee under their own terms and privacy policies. We only receive the details those services share with the people they pay.

Children

FlixView does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you are a parent or guardian with a concern, contact us through GitHub Issues.

Your rights

Because your FlixView data lives on your own computer, you can see, export or delete it at any time from Settings, without asking us.

You also have rights under the data protection laws that apply to you, including the Data Protection Act, 2019 of Kenya. If you have a question or request about your personal information, contact us through GitHub Issues.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. If a change is significant, we will also mention it in the release notes for the version of the app it affects.

Contact

For any question about this policy, open an issue on GitHub Issues.
